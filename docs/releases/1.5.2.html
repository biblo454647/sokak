A smoother sweep, a steadier menu

The wiper now moves on every display frame and temporarily uses your screen's refresh rate, up to 120 Hz, even with Low power enabled. A short motion blur softens the moving blade, while the photograph and water remain sharp. Reusing still backgrounds reduces rendering work.

The control menu keeps its size and menu-bar anchor when switching rain, snow or mist. Clean rain starts, retained water and your wiper shortcut are preserved.
