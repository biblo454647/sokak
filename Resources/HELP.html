sokak

A little weather. A little home.

A moment away

Click the cloud in your menu bar, choose Rain, Snow, or Mist, then Let the weather in. Sound starts softly. You can mute it or adjust its volume independently.

Over my windows puts weather above your work while clicks pass through. A street somewhere fills the display with a real photograph. Click the photograph row to browse 22 photographs from 12 cities or add your own.

While a street is open, press → for the next photograph or ← for the previous one. You can also click the arrows at the bottom corners. The list wraps around, and rain, water on the glass, sound and your sleep timer keep going. With weather matching on, you browse photographs for the current weather; with it off, you browse the entire library. The current photograph stays visible while the next one loads.

Press Escape to leave a photograph. ⌃⌥⌘S starts or pauses from anywhere. ⌃⌥⌘M mutes. Right-click the menu-bar cloud to start or pause. Choose Quit Sokak from the … menu to exit.

Beneath rainy glass

Think of looking up through a shallow roof window. A round drop approaches a point on the glass, taps it, spreads briefly and settles into a water bead. Sometimes a few tiny beads spread around the contact. Water joins its neighbours and drains very slowly. Snow drifts at different distances; a few flakes catch the pane and the edges frost softly.

On a street photograph, droplets bend the street behind them. Over your open windows, Sokak draws glass highlights and trails without reading or distorting other apps. “Window glass” is on by default; switch it off in A few little details if you prefer weather alone.

In photograph mode, Softness adds a little softness while keeping the street recognizable. Move toward Clear for a sharp view. Rain and glass droplets stay visible at every setting.

Make it yours

Rain intensity controls how often drops strike the glass. Individual drops keep their size and approach speed. Each new contact can make a quiet, softly panned tap. Small water beads stay on the glass; larger merged drops can slide away. They no longer disappear on a timer. Wind changes the approach drift. A sleep timer pauses the session after 15, 30, 60, or 120 minutes. “A few little details” also contains Low power, weather matching, dimming, and optional update checks.

A clear pane, whenever you want

Press ⌃⌥⌘W while rain and Window glass are running. A narrow beam wiper makes one out-and-back sweep, gathering water along the rubber edge and carrying it offscreen. Rain keeps falling. The sound switch and volume apply to the quiet wiper strokes as well as rain.

To choose your own keys, open A few little details → Wiper shortcut, click the displayed combination, and press a new one containing Command or Control. Escape cancels. A shortcut already in use keeps your previous setting. The Wipe glass button works without a shortcut. Pressing again during a sweep does not restart it. Snow keeps its existing behavior.

Current display means the display containing your pointer when you start. The session stays there until stopped. Select a named display or All displays if you prefer.

Weather matching chooses wet or overcast photographs for rain and real winter photographs for snow. The library opens with suitable views. All streets shows the complete rainy, snowy and mist collection; explicitly selecting an incompatible photo turns matching off until you enable it again. Your own photographs stay on your Mac.

Installing on a Mac

Sokak needs macOS 13 or later and Metal graphics. The ZIP contains one app for Apple Silicon and Intel. Unzip and keep Sokak.app in your home Applications folder; there is no installer or administrator helper.

The current download is ad-hoc signed and not notarized by Apple. macOS may block its first launch. If you trust the app and your device policy allows it, after attempting to open it use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Managed Macs may need IT approval. Apple's guidance.

Updates

Click Updates at the bottom of the menu. If a new version is available, choose Install Update, then Install and Relaunch. Sokak downloads the release from GitHub, verifies its signature, and preserves your settings and personal photographs. No GitHub account is needed. Keep Sokak.app in a writable Applications folder; read-only locations, app translocation or company policies can prevent replacement.

Version 1.2 and earlier need one manual upgrade from the ZIP to gain this feature. Automatic checks are optional in Details and are off by default. Installation always remains your choice.

Offline, and quiet by default

Weather, sound and photographs work offline. Updates contact GitHub when requested, or while optional automatic checks are enabled. No screen recording, Accessibility, microphone, location, account or analytics is needed. The app starts paused, pauses on screen sleep or session deactivation, and has no login item or scheduled service. Some protected full-screen or system surfaces may stay above the overlay.

Photographs are real images, not live feeds. The files retain their original lighting. Sounds are original synthesized ambience, not recordings from those locations. Photograph credits and licenses.
